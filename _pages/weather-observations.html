These are the raw materials behind the undercast write-up: what the summit cameras saw at local noon, next to what the summit observer wrote down within a few minutes of that frame.

Each card carries three independent verdicts — my own score from the photographs, the observer’s METAR, and the automated screen that reads the METAR. Where they disagree is the interesting part, and it is the reason the hand labels are kept as a test set rather than used for training.

Reading the METAR: the highlighted pieces are the ones the screen looks at — visibility, any layer overhead, and the TPS LWR group giving the coverage and top height of the deck below the 6,288 ft summit. Heights in the body are above the station; TPS LWR heights are above sea level.
